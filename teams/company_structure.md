Company Structure (Managers & Teams)

Executive Managers:
- CEO: Strategy, corporate alignment, final approvals
- CFO: Finance, budgeting, compliance for monetary matters
- CTO: Technical roadmap, architecture, engineering standards
- COO: Operations, product delivery processes
- CHRO: People, hiring, org health
- CDO: Data strategy, data governance
- CPO: Product strategy and prioritization
- CLO: Legal, contracts, IP
- CCO: Customer success and partnerships
- CRO: Revenue, sales engineering
- CSO: Corporate security strategy

Core Engineering & Operations Teams:
- Optimization Team: cross-cutting team to optimize performance, cost, infra and models.
- Security Team: Encryption engineering, cyber ops, blue team, red team, secure development.
- Product Engineering: backend, frontend, API department, native clients, integrations.
- API Department: public/private API contracts, auth, rate limiting, gateway design, versioning, service-to-service integration.
- QA & Testing: functional, regression (red-green), fuzzing, chaos engineering, test automation.
- DevOps & Env: CI/CD, environment handling, secrets, deployment.
- Design & Experience: UI/UX, 3D/4D prototyping, visual design.
- Legal & Compliance: privacy, contracts, regulations.
- Data & ML: data engineering, MLOps, model governance.

Cross-team governance:
- Teams publish machine-readable manifests in teams/manifests to avoid semantic drift.
- Weekly syncs and automated hand-off validations reduce confusion.
